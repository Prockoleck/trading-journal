Email Auto-Import (MT5 Trade Notifications)

Configure your email so the app can parse MT5 trade notifications. In MT5 mobile: Settings → Email → set your email to forward trade confirmations here. The app scans for emails from MetaQuotes / MT5 containing trade details.

Common: imap.gmail.com, imap-mail.outlook.com, imap.mail.yahoo.com
For Gmail: create an App Password. For Outlook: use your regular password or an app password.

How to Set Up MT5 Email Notifications
  1. Open MetaTrader 5 on your phone
  2. Go to Settings → Email
  3. Enter the email address you configured above
  4. Enable notifications for trade signals & order execution
  5. Each trade will send an email → the app will auto-import it
  6. Hit "Import Now" above or the Import Email button on the Trades page
